NO. 4 · WED 12 JUL 2000
A DECISION A DAY · CAN YOU MANAGE THE BAND AND THEIR EGOS?
It's a people game, not a music game

Four band members. Four agendas.
One manager.

Every card is a real decision, and most decisions move at least one meter the wrong way. The studio, the awards toilet queue, the contract on the table — it's the same job underneath: keep four people, a reputation and a bank balance from pulling apart at once.

FOUR METERS, ALWAYS MOVING: EGO MORALE REPUTATION MONEY — soothe the ego and the morale slips; chase the money and the reputation pays for it.

NO. 6 · SAT 7 OCT 2000 · DOORS 8PM
GIG NIGHT · CHART DAY · THE ROAD
The venues are characters too

Saturday is a show.
Sunday is a verdict.

The indie chart. The national chart. And one day, if you hold it together — tour your new album. You climb it a venue at a time, from a pub that takes a fifth of the door to stages that the greats have graced.

ONE WEEK IN THE LIFE

☑ MON–FRI — a decision a day. Band, money, press, ego. Swipe; live with your choices.

☑ SAT — gig night. The lights sweep, the crowd shows (or doesn't), the receipt at the end doesn't lie.

☑ SUN — sales and chart day. News from the label on the week's sales — to make, or quietly end, your week.

As the season turns: Awards, Battle of the Bands, a Christmas #1 race — and a slow climb from the toilet circuit toward the kind of room you used to dream about.

NO. 9 · DEC 2002

Who is making it — and the ask.

ABOUT ME

David Hawkins is a solo developer with a systems-engineering background, old enough to have lived the era the game is set in. Design, writing and code are all his — and all human-led, with commissioned art replacing the current placeholders so there's no AI-generated art at ship. The way he works is half the pitch: a bug reported in the morning is often fixed and live to testers by the afternoon.

Solo output Small-team velocity Low burn
CURRENT STATE

Formation and Rising play end to end right now — live on iOS and Android at the v0.9.0 preview, with an economy tuned against a thousand simulation runs. Roughly six months from content-complete: Peak and Legacy content, commissioned art and music, and a Steam / Switch 2 port assessment to follow, with landscape mode already in development.

MEMORANDUM OF TERMS · THE ASK

A publishing partnership. ~6 months' development funding to content-complete + 6 months' supported post-launch. Budgets for commissioned art (31 artists × 4 emotions, 50+ backgrounds), real music for in-game audio tracks, QA at scale, marketing that knows how to sell craft, and legal expertise around artist likenesses.

  1. A proven format (Reigns: 1.8M+) executed with genuine writing quality
  2. An unoccupied market position — no card-swipe music management game exists
  3. A developer who ships fixes within hours, at solo cost
  4. Premium — one purchase, no ads, no energy bars, no predatory anything
  5. An era-agnostic engine: sequels and licensed editions by design
BEYOND THE FIRST RECORD

The engine is the asset. The card system, meters and band narrative are era-agnostic — the same tech carries a 1960s edition (British Invasion, mod, psychedelia), a modern streaming-age edition, or licensed editions telling a real band's story. Reigns proved the licensing model works (Game of Thrones, The Witcher); this is the proof of concept for music. The first record comes first — but the catalogue is built in.
